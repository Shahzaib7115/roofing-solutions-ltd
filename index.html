What the weather does to your roof

British weather quickly causes leaks, cracked or missing slates, damaged tiles and failing flat roofs. Heavy rain, strong winds and storms expose weak points in pitched roofs, chimneys, flashing and guttering, leading to water ingress and rising repair costs.

Without proper repairs, small problems escalate into structural damage, higher energy bills and safety risks. Early inspection and professional repair protect your property.

FAQ

Roofing Questions, Answered

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. Inspections and quotes are free, with no obligation.

What causes roof leaks?

Storm damage, ageing materials, poor installation or maintenance, blocked gutters and failed flashing are the most common causes. We find the real source before we repair anything.

Repair or replace?

Many problems can be repaired. The age of the roof and the extent of the damage decide, and we always give you honest advice rather than pushing a full replacement.

How much does a small roof repair cost?

Most small repairs cost between £150 and £600. A few slipped slates can be as little as £95. Every quote is free and in writing.

How long does a repair take?

A few hours for small jobs, several days for extensive work. We tell you the timescale before we start.

Which flat roof material is best?

GRP fibreglass and EPDM rubber are the most durable options for most homes, with long guarantees and low maintenance.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured with public liability cover. Certificates available on request.

Do you work in winter?

Yes. Some repairs take longer in cold weather and need different materials, but we work all year round, including emergency call-outs.
